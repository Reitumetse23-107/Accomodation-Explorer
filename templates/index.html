From scattered listings to one clear comparison

Prices in Francistown are hard to judge when every place is listed on a different site. We put them side by side and measure each one against what similar stays typically cost.

Collect

We gathered listings with price, distance from the city centre, review score, review count, room type and star rating.

2

Estimate

A regression model, tested with leave-one-out validation, estimates a fair nightly price for each listing.

3

Compare

Every listing is flagged under-priced, fair or over-priced, then scored and ranked so you can filter and compare side by side.
